# FICTIONAL SAMPLE — Terms and FAQ

## Payment milestones
40% on written acceptance and kickoff.
30% on approval of the agreed intermediate deliverables.
30% on final acceptance and handover.

## Proposal validity
The proposal is valid for 30 days from its issue date.
The actual issue date is To be confirmed.

## Scope and change control
The final scope and acceptance criteria require written agreement.
Additional requirements require a written change request.

## Exclusions
Software subscriptions, third-party licences, travel, hosting, and data
migration are excluded unless expressly included in the agreed scope.

## Customer responsibilities
The customer supplies an authorised project owner, timely feedback,
required access, and suitable source data.

## Schedule
Service timelines are planning assumptions. The final schedule depends
on confirmed scope, access, dependencies, and customer availability.

## Tax
The user supplies the tax percentage. The application performs arithmetic
only and does not determine the correct tax treatment.

## Outcomes
No revenue increase, conversion improvement, implementation date, or
other business outcome is guaranteed.
