# FICTIONAL SAMPLE — Illustrative project patterns

These examples are invented for classroom use. They are not actual customer
engagements and must not be presented as proven outcomes.

## Manufacturing scenario
A fictional manufacturer wants a consistent way to record enquiries,
assign sales owners, and review open opportunities.

An illustrative scope includes a requirements workshop, CRM pipeline
configuration, a reporting dashboard, and user training.

Actual adoption, implementation effort, and commercial outcomes would
depend on data quality, agreed scope, and customer participation.

## Professional-services scenario
A fictional professional-services company wants clearer opportunity
tracking and a shared review process.

An illustrative scope includes discovery, workflow configuration, training,
and a support period.

No quantitative results, named customers, or verified testimonials are
available.
